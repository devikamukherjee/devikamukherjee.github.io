About

Hello, I'm Devika.

I'm a Research Associate at the University of Birmingham working on wearable haptics, VR and agentic XR, and human-AI interaction — studying how feedback people can feel, and systems that propose before they act, make immersive technology safer and more trustworthy.

I own the whole research cycle: designing and building the prototype, running the controlled study, analysing behavioural and self-report data in Python, and turning it into evidence-based design recommendations. That work led to a first-author paper at CHI 2026, a 16-participant study of real-time VR world generation, and a CHI 2027 submission on laser-printed graphene interfaces.

Belt and Whistles: Adding Lower Body Collision Awareness for MR Experiences

Mukherjee, D., Abdlkarim, D., Giunchi, D., Di Luca, M., Ofek, E. (2026)

Proceedings of the ACM CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems (CHI '26) · University of Birmingham · Microsoft Research

A wearable haptic belt that extends mixed-reality collision awareness below the user's field of view, where lower-body obstacles fall outside visual attention. Controlled studies with 35+ participants compared multisensory feedback strategies for embodiment, task performance and perceived safety, and produced a non-auditory, hands-free channel for spatial and safety information.

Healthcare HCI · Clinical decision support · Streamlit

Signal or Noise

A clinician triage interface for remote heart-failure monitoring under alert overload. The classic rule (more than 2 kg gained in three days) fires constantly on ordinary weight fluctuation, so a nurse watching 200 patients wades through alerts that are mostly nothing. The project treats nurse attention as the binding constraint and asks a design question: given a nurse can work through N alerts a morning, which alerting strategy finds the most patients who are actually decompensating?

Synthetic longitudinal cohort, three alerting rules swept across thresholds and compared at matched alert burden, a worklist interface built around the nurse's attention budget, and an audit of AI-written rationales for hallucinated numbers, flipped directions and unsupported claims. A pre-registered 2×2 evaluation protocol with human participants is written and waiting to run.

LLM evaluation · Reliability audit · Python

Can an LLM be a trustworthy voting-advice app?

Voting Advice Applications like Germany's Wahl-O-Mat are deterministic arithmetic: encode voter and party positions as vectors, return the nearest party. This harness swaps the arithmetic for a language model and measures what breaks — and because the arithmetic answer is always computable, every case has a ground truth.

Five research questions on 2025 federal-election data (38 theses × 28 parties): accuracy, stability across repeats, order sensitivity, sycophancy to an irrelevant identity cue, and the headline — does accuracy drop when party names are anonymised and the model can no longer lean on memorised priors? A 25 × 6 × 5 factorial design, modal aggregation with explicit tie handling, bootstrap confidence intervals resampled at the profile level, content-hash caching and append-only JSONL logs. Metrics were fixed before any result was seen.

UX research · Mixed methods · Social-media analysis

      
TikTok Shop on Reddit

      
TikTok Shop users don't fill in usability surveys on their own initiative, but they do post to Reddit about scams, refunds and delighted "found this on my FYP" moments. A reproducible pipeline treats that organic discussion as a low-cost complement to moderated research: collection, cleaning and anonymisation, transparent rule-based theme coding, VADER sentiment, and report-ready figures and tables.

      
The coding scheme is a human-readable, auditable set of keyword rules rather than a black-box classifier, with a 30-item random sample exported for manual validation. Usernames are dropped at collection, text is scrubbed of mentions and contact details, and published quotes are paraphrased to reduce re-identification risk. The pipeline is verified end to end; the live Reddit pull is pending API access.
